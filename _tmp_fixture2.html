Excalibur is a Tenno warrior known for his master swordsmanship and well-rounded combat abilities.

Slash Dash impales enemies, Radial Blind blinds enemies, while Radial Javelin launches javelins and Exalted Blade summons an ethereal greatsword.

Acquisition

[edit]

Excalibur is one of the three starter Warframes, alongside Mag and Volt, during Awakening.

Excalibur's main blueprint can be purchased from the Market.
